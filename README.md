# Huluul District: Integrated Water, Agriculture & Food Security Project

## Overview
This project presents a comprehensive development framework for Huluul District, Sanaag. The core mission is to transform the district from a water-scarce region into a self-sufficient agricultural hub by tapping into untapped groundwater resources and integrating modern farming techniques.

## Strategic Vision: The Water-Agriculture Nexus
We believe that water access is the foundation of economic growth. By providing sustainable water sources, we unlock the potential for:
* **Climate-Smart Agriculture:** Transitioning nomadic and rural communities toward crop production.
* **Food Sovereignty:** Reducing reliance on imported food through local irrigation.
* **Economic Resilience:** Strengthening the production of gums, resins, and minerals.

## Key Project Components
1. **Groundwater Exploration:** Drilling of shallow (35m) and strategic deep wells.
2. **Infrastructure Distribution:** Establishing water-hubs within a 35km radius to cover urban, village, and nomadic areas.
3. **Agricultural Integration:** Provision of high-quality seeds, modern tools, and technical training.
4. **Livestock Support:** Dedicated watering points to protect pastoralist livelihoods during droughts.

## Objectives
* To eliminate water trucking dependency in the 18 villages surrounding Huluul.
* To establish permanent irrigation systems for small-scale farming.
* To improve community health through clean, fresh water access.
