# Project Implementation Plan: Huluul Water & Agriculture

This plan outlines the four critical phases required to move from data analysis to field execution.

## Phase 1: Site Validation & Hydrogeological Survey (Months 1-2)
* Deploy a technical team to Huluul to conduct geophysical surveys.
* Identify exact coordinates for the 35m shallow wells based on groundwater potential.
* Engage with local elders and village committees for site approval.

## Phase 2: Drilling & Infrastructure Development (Months 3-8)
* Contracting drilling rigs for the 18 identified villages.
* Installation of solar-powered submersible pumps to ensure low-cost operations.
* Construction of elevated water tanks and distribution kiosks.

## Phase 3: Agricultural Launch (Months 6-12)
* Distribution of the 'Huluul Seed Kit' (Tomatoes, Onions, Fodder, etc.).
* Setting up 35km-radius demonstration plots to train nomadic farmers.
* Installation of drip irrigation systems to conserve water.

## Phase 4: Monitoring & Sustainability (Ongoing)
* Establishing "Water Committees" for each well to manage maintenance.
* Quarterly water quality testing and soil health analysis.
