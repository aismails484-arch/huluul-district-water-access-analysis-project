# Water Access Report – Huluul District

## Key Findings
- Nomadic communities have the longest distance to water
- Urban areas have better access but still face limitations
- Villages experience moderate challenges

## Challenges
- Lack of freshwater wells
- Dependence on berkado
- Limited infrastructure

## Recommendations
- Develop wells where feasible
- Expand berkads where wells are not possible
- Use berkads as storage systems
