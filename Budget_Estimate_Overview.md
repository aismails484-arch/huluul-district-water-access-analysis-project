# Phase 1: Groundwater Infrastructure Development (Total: $400,000)

The primary objective of Phase 1 is the successful drilling, testing, and commissioning of water sources across Huluul District. This phase ensures the water foundation is secure before proceeding to agriculture.

| Component | Detailed Allocation | Estimated Cost (USD) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rig Mobilization & Drilling** | Heavy machinery transport and drilling of 18 sites | $210,000 |
| **Casing & Well Lining** | High-quality PVC/Steel casing to ensure well longevity | $50,000 |
| **Pumping Systems** | Solar-powered submersible pumps for each well | $70,000 |
| **Water Quality Testing** | Laboratory analysis for salinity and biological safety | $15,000 |
| **Site Preparation** | Clearing, fencing, and initial concrete work | $25,000 |
| **Technical Supervision** | Hydrogeologists and Drilling Engineers (Field Staff) | $30,000 |
| **TOTAL PHASE 1** | | **$400,000** |
