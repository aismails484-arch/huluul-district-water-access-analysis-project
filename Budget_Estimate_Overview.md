# Budget Estimate Overview (Optimized Scale-Up)

## Phase 1: Groundwater Infrastructure Development & Storage Network (Total: $2,000,000)

The primary objective of Phase 1 has been scaled to a $2,000,000 budget to establish a robust, reliable, and sustainable water foundation across 18 sites in Huluul District. This mid-tier allocation optimizes standard drilling operations by introducing high-capacity solar systems, centralized community storage reservoirs, and advanced water purification to effectively bridge the gap between basic water access and agricultural readiness.

### Key Adjustments in the $2M Budget Optimization

1. **Enhanced Drilling Depth (+$740K Increase):** Expands the drilling capabilities to ensure all 18 sites reach deeper, year-round aquifers rather than shallower seasonal water tables, minimizing drought risks.
2. **Dedicated Storage Infrastructure ($230K):** Introduces high-capacity elevated storage tanks to manage water pressure and hold reliable reserves, ensuring water is readily available for community use and future agricultural expansion.
3. **Advanced Solar Grids ($320K):** Upgrades standard pumping mechanisms to heavy-duty solar systems that maximize water output during peak sunlight hours without incurring ongoing fuel or electrical costs.
4. **Water Safety & Filtration ($80K):** Integrates specialized multi-stage filtration units at sites requiring treatment, ensuring that the water quality remains safe for both soil biology and human consumption.
