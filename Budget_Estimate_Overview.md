# Budget Estimate Overview (Updated)

The primary objective of Phase 1 has been scaled up to secure long-term, high-capacity water access across 18 strategic sites in Huluul District. This updated estimate ensures a resilient water foundation, advanced solar technology, heavy-duty storage, and treatment infrastructure to fully support both domestic supply and large-scale agricultural transition.

### Key Adjustments in the $5M Budget Scale-Up

1. **Deep Borehole Focus:** Allocates substantial funds to reach deeper, more reliable aquifers that guarantee an uninterrupted year-round water supply independent of seasonal drought cycles.
2. **Integrated Storage & Agri-Distribution:** Adds $600,000 dedicated entirely to building elevated bulk reservoirs, eliminating water waste and laying down immediate pipeline pathways into adjacent farming blocks.
3. **Advanced Filtration & Desalination:** Dedicates $200,000 for advanced water treatment. This mitigates salinity risks, ensuring that extracted groundwater remains optimal for sustainable agricultural soil profiles.
4. **Resilient Power Grids:** The solar system budget was upgraded to include smart-grid capabilities, ensuring maximum energy efficiency during peak and off-peak sunlight hours.
